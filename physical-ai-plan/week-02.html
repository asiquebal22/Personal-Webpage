The daily plan

Day 1 starts on your selected program date; the labels are work sessions, not fixed weekdays. Divide each session using the week’s hour ledger.

DAY 01

GAE and PPO

Implement returns/GAE and the PPO update. Check terminal versus truncated trajectories manually. Inspect CleanRL's rollout-buffer layout.

DAY 02

A complete RL run

Run a small reference PPO experiment and save all hyperparameters. Inspect losses, entropy, approximate KL and gradient norms. Fix wrapper issues before tuning learning rates.

DAY 03

SAC and replay

Derive the soft Q target and inspect a reference SAC trainer. Run a continuous-control smoke experiment. Explain replay reuse, target networks and tanh action scaling.

DAY 04

Robot data pathway

Finish observation/action logging and the reset procedure. Acquire a few nominal and obstructed diagnostic rollouts. Confirm both success and failure labels are observable.

DAY 05

Read the optimization foundations

Read the original PPO and SAC objectives and connect them to your implementation. Survey DAgger for the distribution-shift problem; keep the project-specific adaptation papers for their assigned later weeks.

DAY 06

Gate 1 review

Demonstrate reset→observe→act→record→replay in the actual task. Present a tensor-level PPO explanation and choose the continuous-control implementation to reuse.

DAY 07

Recover or absorb overflow

Keep this day flexible. Optional reading or a delayed run can fit here; do not assume another full mandatory workday.
