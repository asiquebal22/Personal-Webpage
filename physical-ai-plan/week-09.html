Week 09 / Evidence

Run targeted ablations and interrogate failure

Understand the mechanism and close the most consequential experimental gaps.

42 core hours
8 h Theory3 h Coding labs23 h Research8 h Interview / career

The daily plan

Day 1 starts on your selected program date; the labels are work sessions, not fixed weekdays. Divide each session using the week’s hour ledger.

DAY 01

Prioritize three ablations: planning versus policy updates; frozen versus corrected world model; active versus random trials. Add one only when it answers a concrete remaining question.

DAY 02

Compare short versus longer imagination under the same compute budget. Inspect whether apparent improvement is concentrated where prediction errors are large.

DAY 03

Measure subsequent encounters and nominal-task retention. Test whether learned changes amortize their training time across repeated blockage scenarios.

DAY 04

Review raw trajectories, outcome labels and unsafe/aborted attempts. Check whether the method succeeded by exploiting a reward or evaluation loophole.

DAY 05

Practice designing the complete deployed loop, including model versioning, timers, reset overhead and rollback. Continue targeted applications and follow-ups.

DAY 06

Prepare the figure list and evidence-backed contribution statement. Recheck closely related papers before deciding what is new.

DAY 07

Recover or absorb overflow

Keep this day flexible. Optional reading or a delayed run can fit here; do not assume another full mandatory workday.
