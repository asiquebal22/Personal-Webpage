Study checklist

Papers, reading order and priorities

Check a paper after producing a note at its assigned depth. Work through critical items first; time-permitting and optional readings are not extra obligations.

Critical Protect this work first: interview foundations, the core adaptation experiment and a trustworthy artifact. Complete the stated depth; survey does not mean full reproduction.

If time permits Do after the critical work for that week is on track. Use the existing timebox; replace lower-value review rather than extending the week.

Optional An extension or specialist direction. It is not required to finish the three-month program and does not hold up release.

Priority and depth are different: a critical survey requires understanding the mechanism and limitation; a deep read also requires the objective, method and evidence. It does not require reproducing the full system.

Three connected lines of development

Models and decisions

Dyna → World Models → PETS / PlaNet → Dreamer / MBPO

Learn dynamics, then distinguish planning through them from learning a policy with them. Continue to DreamerV3, TD-MPC2, DINO-WM and V-JEPA 2-AC.

Robot policies and data

DAgger → ACT / Diffusion Policy → RT-1 / RT-2 → generalist VLAs

Study distribution shift, multimodal actions, scaling robot data and VLM transfer. Compare Open X-Embodiment, Octo, OpenVLA/OFT and π₀.

Foundation world and action models

Transformers / generative models → Cosmos → Cosmos Policy / DreamZero

Connect representation and generation to actual robot actions. Study the current Cosmos 3 architecture and checkpoint interfaces.

These are conceptual learning paths, not claims that each paper directly descends from its neighbor or that one family supersedes another.

Deep read · Model-based RL · 1991 · Primary paper ↗

Mechanism and implementation connection

Richard S. Sutton

Mechanism: Interleave learning from interaction with planning updates using a learned model.

Do: Draw the real-data/model-data/update loop. Extend the gridworld with a learned tabular transition table and compare equal real-transition budgets.

Mechanism and implementation connection

Mechanism: A compressed visual representation and recurrent dynamics support a compact controller; a policy can be trained in the learned dream environment.

What counts as understanding a paper?

  1. Draw the observations, actions, model, actor and learning signals, marking what is learned versus supplied.
  2. Write the main objective and explain each term, including where gradients stop.
  3. Locate one result that supports the central claim and one limitation relevant to your robot.
  4. Connect the paper to a working exercise or a precise implementation inspection.
  5. Explain what changed relative to its predecessor and what your project still needs to demonstrate.

This page provides study prompts and verified primary links. It does not claim that every paper has been reproduced or that abstracts establish detailed experimental validity. Use the six-question note for full-paper reading.
