The daily plan

Day 1 starts on your selected program date; the labels are work sessions, not fixed weekdays. Divide each session using the week’s hour ledger.

DAY 01

Resolve the most important missing comparison or uncertainty issue. Rerun only affected experiments and keep previous results versioned.

DAY 02

Repeat critical real trials if necessary. Verify that released videos and figures correspond to documented checkpoints, configurations and data.

DAY 03

Remove credentials and private records, document data/model licenses, and create a small redistributable sample dataset with provenance.

DAY 04

Run one research deep dive and one coding/system-design mock. Write missed questions into a focused review list.

DAY 05

Adapt the project presentation to robot learning, world models or evaluation roles. Research each team's technical focus before interviews.

DAY 06

Confirm the release checklist, paper claims, rerun instructions and known failures. Choose a realistic submission route based on evidence and verified venue dates.

DAY 07

Recover or absorb overflow

Keep this day flexible. Optional reading or a delayed run can fit here; do not assume another full mandatory workday.
