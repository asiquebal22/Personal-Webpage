The daily plan

Day 1 starts on your selected program date; the labels are work sessions, not fixed weekdays. Divide each session using the week’s hour ledger.

DAY 01

Detect and represent blockage

Define stagnation/collision/progress checks on development data. Reobserve before adaptation; distinguish current latent-state inference from changing model weights.

DAY 02

Generate candidates

Build a legal correction-trial pool independent of held-out tests. Compare predicted outcomes across ensemble members and compute an initial disagreement score.

DAY 03

Verify physically

Run a small random correction batch, add executed transitions, update the world model between rounds, then repeat imagined actor adaptation. Count reset and human time.

DAY 04

Read related correction work

Read Fail2Progress's targeted-data method and constrained-shelf qualitative result. Read ReSYNC's skill/concept learning and curriculum assumptions. Update the novelty matrix.

DAY 05

Preregister comparisons

Fix primary methods, budgets, seeds, evaluation layout IDs, success rubric, time caps and stopping rules. Narrow the claim if the pilot shows replanning is sufficient.

DAY 06

Gate 3 and private rehearsal

Create a private 5–10 minute research presentation and a clean pilot demo. Finish the CV/project summary and target-role list; do not send outreach until Week 7.

DAY 07

Recover or absorb overflow

Keep this day flexible. Optional reading or a delayed run can fit here; do not assume another full mandatory workday.
