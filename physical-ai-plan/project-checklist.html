01 · Questions and claims

QuestionWhat to establish
RQ1 · Measure the imagination–execution gapDo predicted gains survive execution after a new obstruction? Use paired starts and actual outcomes; a large gap is a possible finding, not a required result.
RQ2 · Establish the value of adaptationDo persistent policy updates add anything beyond frozen behavior and compute-matched planning with the same learned model?
RQ3 · Spend real trials wellAt a fixed correction budget, does active selection beat uniform random trials? A policy-rollout refresh baseline is the next comparison if time permits.
RQ4 · Test transfer and retentionDo corrections help on untouched obstruction layouts, and does the nominal task still work after adaptation?

02 · Study and prior work

Start with the prioritized paper list. Add the attachment’s reliability papers and active-learning foundations at the assigned depth. Read mechanisms and evidence before adopting an algorithm.

03 · Setup and protocol

Primary setup: one arm, one obstruction-induced strategy-change task, logged execution and independent outcome checks. Two tasks and shared policy/simulator weights are extensions after the pilot.

04 · Data and real-trial budget

Initial hardware envelope: 320–430 episodes, including development, correction, evaluation and retention. The simulation plan begins with 1,080 headline evaluations across methods, seeds and layouts. These are provisional allocations; measure reset time and data needs before collection. Full budget and accounting →

05 · Models and learning loop

Critical training includes the nominal policy, learned dynamics and actual imagined policy updates. Foundation-model interface knowledge is critical; a full Cosmos/DreamZero or generative-policy training experiment is conditional on compatibility and time.

06 · Experiments, one question per figure

One main figure per question. Report negative results and raw denominators. A desired optimism gap or success increase is a hypothesis, not a condition for accepting an experiment.

07 · Gates and schedule

Use week-relative gates rather than the attachment’s older calendar dates. Dates follow the start date in the plan home. Protect writing/figure-review time inside the research allocation from Week 4.
