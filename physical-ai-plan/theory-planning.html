Planning, control and policy adaptation

When is searching for a new action sequence enough, and when is learning worthwhile?

Research core10–14 study hoursConnected lab 09 →

What to understand

  • Receding-horizon control; CEM, random shooting and MPPI at a conceptual level.
  • Configuration-space obstacles, RRT intuition, contact modes, non-prehensile manipulation and geometric feasibility.
  • Task/skill sequencing versus continuous action optimization; symbolic versus neural world models.
  • Transient replanning versus persistent policy updates; retention, transfer, compute amortization and total recovery latency.

What to derive or explain

  1. Write an MPC objective with goal progress, control cost and constraint costs, and identify the horizon and terminal term.
  2. Derive CEM's elite mean/variance update and explain the warm start.
  3. Explain a case in which moving away from the goal is necessary to eventually reach it.

Connect it to code

Build CEM-MPC on the same learned dynamics used by the adaptation agent. Keep model calls, horizon, initial observations and wall-clock budget visible. Add an exact-physics oracle only as a diagnostic upper bound.
