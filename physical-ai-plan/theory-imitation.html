Imitation, offline RL and robot data

How do demonstrations become a robust starting policy?

Core10–14 study hoursConnected lab 06 →

What to understand

  • Behavior cloning; covariate shift; DAgger and corrective demonstrations.
  • Offline RL, out-of-distribution action values, conservatism, and why arbitrary online updates can destroy a good offline policy.
  • Episode boundaries, action normalization, camera alignment, observation history and dataset splitting by scene or episode.
  • Chunked actions, execution horizons, observation horizons and the control trade-off between smoothness and reactivity.

What to derive or explain

  1. Write BC as maximum likelihood and distinguish single-mode regression from multimodal action distributions.
  2. Explain the source of offline Q-value extrapolation error without memorizing an algorithm name.
  3. Design a split that prevents neighboring video frames leaking between training and evaluation.

Connect it to code

Train a BC baseline on unobstructed simulated task demonstrations. Hold out complete layouts and inspect failure videos. Then measure the distribution shift caused by introducing a blocker. Save normalization statistics with the checkpoint.
