The daily plan

Day 1 starts on your selected program date; the labels are work sessions, not fixed weekdays. Divide each session using the week’s hour ledger.

DAY 01

Prepare a concise CV, one-page research summary and preliminary demo. Identify five well-matched teams. Send a small, tailored first batch yourself; track feedback separately from research outcomes.

DAY 02

Run active and random selection from the same candidate generator. Allocate two research hours to finish the foundation-model capability/latency audit. Make an explicit decision about a compatible large-model extension; keep the primary methods unchanged unless the protocol is versioned.

DAY 03

Evaluate frozen policy, same-model MPC, imagined adaptation and adaptation with random/active correction. Use the prespecified development/evaluation separation.

DAY 04

Repeat simulation runs with independent initializations and training seeds. Log compute and adaptation time. Run a limited hardware feasibility block where appropriate.

DAY 05

Practice tensor/replay-buffer coding and a 10-minute project explanation. Explain the strongest baseline and what result would invalidate the method.

DAY 06

Generate a preliminary real-success-versus-correction-budget plot with visible uncertainty and sample counts. Tag it preliminary; record all discrepancies and failures.

DAY 07

Recover or absorb overflow

Keep this day flexible. Optional reading or a delayed run can fit here; do not assume another full mandatory workday.
