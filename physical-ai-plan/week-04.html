The daily plan

Day 1 starts on your selected program date; the labels are work sessions, not fixed weekdays. Divide each session using the week’s hour ledger.

DAY 01

Action-conditioned validity

Test zero, permuted and deliberately colliding actions. Check whether prediction errors preserve or erase the obstacle. Add failure data only from training/development scenes.

DAY 02

CEM-MPC

Implement receding-horizon search on the learned model. Log horizon, model calls, elapsed time and the action prefix executed. Keep exact simulator dynamics out of this baseline.

DAY 03

Generative-policy mini-lab

Begin the two-route action denoiser/flow example; finish it alongside the policy readings in Week 5. Separate policy generation from world-model prediction.

DAY 04

Read imagination and model usage

Read Dreamer and MBPO deeply. Contrast analytic actor learning through latent imagination with short synthetic replay rollouts. Survey DreamerV3 and TD-MPC2; label the variant your code implements.

DAY 05

Model and planner evaluation

Run nominal and obstructed development trials. Compare frozen policy, random shooting and learned-model MPC. Identify whether failures are model, search or controller failures.

DAY 06

Gate 2 review

Produce a short paired imagination/execution clip and a planner result. Set a small, measured adaptation-time budget and choose a residual actor implementation for Week 5.

DAY 07

Recover or absorb overflow

Keep this day flexible. Optional reading or a delayed run can fit here; do not assume another full mandatory workday.
