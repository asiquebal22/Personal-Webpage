Lab 07 / Week 4–5

A multimodal action generator

Connect your generative-modeling background to robot action distributions without spending the month training a foundation model.

5 focused hoursMiniature implementationTheory companion →

The implementation sequence

  1. Create a synthetic dataset with two valid routes around an obstacle.
  2. Train a conditional denoiser or a tiny flow-matching vector field over action chunks; connect the objective to the assigned DDPM/flow reading.
  3. Compare samples against an MSE regressor whose mean may cross the obstacle.
  4. Inspect Diffusion Policy, original OpenVLA, OpenVLA-OFT and openpi action interfaces. Continue into the separately budgeted six-hour VLA practice; one actual checkpoint route is sufficient.

How to verify it

  • Plot sampled trajectories and collision rates; visual quality alone is insufficient.
  • Explain the training objective and sampling loop unaided.
  • Separate observation horizon, prediction horizon and executed action prefix.

What to produce

A small action-distribution figure and a two-minute explanation suitable for an interview.
