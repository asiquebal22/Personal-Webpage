Cosmos belongs in the core knowledge map

Study the original platform paper for context, then the Cosmos 3 report and current repository. NVIDIA’s current documentation distinguishes reasoning and generation interfaces and includes action-related capabilities. Do not treat a generic video demo as the complete robotics interface.

Cosmos Policy is a required deep read: it connects video pretraining to robot actions, future-state prediction and values. Compare direct execution with planning, then ask separately how you would fine-tune a policy on imagined experience and verify its gains physically.

For the legacy Predict / Transfer / Reason families, understand prediction versus spatially controlled generation versus reasoning. Newer Cosmos 3 capabilities make a blanket claim that “Cosmos cannot accept actions” incorrect; checkpoint-specific interfaces and embodiment support decide what can actually be used.

OpenVLA is required, alongside its predecessors and alternatives

Read OpenVLA after RT-1, RT-2 and Open X-Embodiment. Trace the original tokenized action decoder and normalization; then compare OpenVLA-OFT and its continuous chunked-action recipe with π₀’s flow-based action generation. Survey ACT, Octo, π₀.₅ and GR00T to understand alternative architecture/data choices.

A VLA is a policy family; a world model supplies predictions of how actions change the environment. A system can contain both. Joint action/video prediction in Cosmos Policy or DreamZero should be examined explicitly rather than inferred from the word “foundation.”

Make a model decision you can defend

  1. Write the exact observation/action contract and compatible embodiment, not only the model name.
  2. Separate source inspection, inference run, fine-tuning smoke test and actual closed-loop evaluation in your report.
  3. Profile memory, rollout throughput and end-to-end latency before promising online imagination at foundation-model scale.
  4. Require action-sensitive predictions at contact and a valid reward/readout before synthetic policy optimization.
  5. Keep the compact core when it answers the research question. Add a foundation-model experiment before making a foundation-model-specific publication claim.
