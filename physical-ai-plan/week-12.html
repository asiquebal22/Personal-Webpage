Week 12 / Release

Release the artifact and complete the interview transition

Deliver a complete public research artifact and communicate the work with precision.

42 core hours
4 h Theory0 h Coding labs24 h Research14 h Interview / career

The daily plan

Day 1 starts on your selected program date; the labels are work sessions, not fixed weekdays. Divide each session using the week’s hour ledger.

DAY 01

Tag a reproducible release, archive evaluation manifests and verify that the repository runs from its documented setup.

DAY 02

Release code/configurations, paired examples and a technical report. Publish a preprint only if authorship, evidence and relevant institutional requirements are resolved.

DAY 03

Share the final artifact with relevant existing contacts. Keep the claim proportional to the number of tasks, layouts, seeds and physical trials.

DAY 04

Repeat the weakest coding, RL or robotics topics from mock interviews. Practice concise answers with concrete examples from the project.

DAY 05

Write a one-page continuation plan covering richer contact, model fidelity, additional embodiments and efficient policy adaptation.

DAY 06

Audit what you can derive, implement, explain and reproduce. Update career materials with completed results rather than planned achievements.

DAY 07

Recover or absorb overflow

Keep this day flexible. Optional reading or a delayed run can fit here; do not assume another full mandatory workday.
