Research question

When an unseen obstruction defeats a robot policy, can imagined policy updates improve executed recovery, and can a small, actively selected budget of real trials correct the model more effectively than random trials?

Start with one task and a feasible action-conditioned model. Compare policy adaptation with replanning; select claims from measured outcomes.
