PPO, SAC and stable optimization

Which policy update is appropriate for continuous robot actions?

Critical14–18 study hoursConnected lab 03 →

What to understand

  • PPO: rollout collection, old log-probabilities, clipped objective, value loss, entropy and advantage normalization.
  • SAC: twin critics, target networks, replay, entropy temperature and reparameterized tanh-Gaussian actions.
  • Why clipping is not a hard trust-region guarantee; why off-policy learning is attractive when robot samples are expensive.
  • Exploding values, observation normalization, reward scale, update-to-data ratio and gradient/debugging diagnostics.

What to derive or explain

  1. Compute the PPO objective for positive and negative advantages with probability ratios outside the clip interval.
  2. Write the SAC target with entropy correction and explain the minimum of two critics.
  3. Derive the tanh log-density correction and explain why you must not backpropagate through target values.

Connect it to code

Implement a small PPO update yourself; use CleanRL to inspect a complete trainer. For SAC, reproduce and modify a maintained implementation rather than rebuilding another full trainer. Compare learning curves and failure modes across seeds.
