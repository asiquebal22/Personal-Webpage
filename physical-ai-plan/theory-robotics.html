Robotics foundations

What connects a policy output to motion of a real end effector?

Core14–18 study hoursConnected lab 02 →

What to understand

  • Reference frames; SE(3), rotation matrices, quaternions, transform composition and camera-to-robot calibration.
  • Forward and inverse kinematics; Jacobians, singularities, joint limits and task-space versus joint-space commands.
  • Joint position, velocity and torque control; Cartesian impedance; where a learned policy sits above the controller.
  • Observations, controller frequency, action duration, latency and synchronization. Review dynamics conceptually rather than spending weeks deriving every manipulator equation.

What to derive or explain

  1. Compose camera→base and object→camera transforms to obtain an object pose in the robot frame.
  2. Derive the planar two-link Jacobian and a damped least-squares IK update.
  3. Explain why the same Cartesian delta can produce very different joint motion near a singularity.

Connect it to code

Write forward kinematics and a Jacobian, verify derivatives numerically, and command an end-effector delta in simulation. Keep units and frame conventions in a single configuration file.
