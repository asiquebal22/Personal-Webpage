Uncertainty, active correction and evaluation

Which few physical trials most improve the next recovery?

Critical12–16 study hoursConnected lab 11 →

What to understand

  • Epistemic versus aleatoric uncertainty; ensembles; disagreement versus calibrated uncertainty.
  • Active learning under policy-induced distribution shift; uncertainty sampling versus expected decision improvement.
  • Model optimism, failure discrimination, Brier score, success reliability and predicted-versus-observed outcomes.
  • Paired comparisons, bootstrap intervals, layout-level independence, seed variation, cost accounting and negative results.

What to derive or explain

  1. Define predicted-minus-observed success gap without assuming the cause is intentional self-deception.
  2. Write one simple acquisition score using predicted success and ensemble disagreement; specify candidate-generation and normalization.
  3. Derive a Bernoulli standard error and explain why many trials from one layout are not many independent tasks.

Connect it to code

Compare active selection with random selection from the same candidate pool and the same real-trial budget. Fit score weights on development data only. Keep the final evaluation set inaccessible to trial selection and stopping decisions.

Interview retrieval practice

  • Can all ensemble members confidently share the same error?
  • What does active selection optimize, and what would falsify its benefit?
  • Why can a lower optimism gap coexist with a worse success rate?
