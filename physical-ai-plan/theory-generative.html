Generative robot policies and VLAs

What do diffusion, flow matching and a VLA change about the action distribution?

Core concepts · optional full training8–12 study hoursConnected lab 07 →

What to understand

  • Denoising over action chunks; timestep conditioning; noise schedules and iterative action generation.
  • Conditional flow matching; transport from noise to actions; training objective versus inference ODE steps.
  • VLA perception-language-action interfaces, proprioception, action representations and embodiment dependence.
  • Fine-tuning heads/adapters versus the whole model; compute, data and inference-latency constraints.

What to derive or explain

  1. Explain the conditional diffusion noise-prediction loss and its inputs.
  2. Construct a straight-line conditional flow-matching target between noise and demonstrated actions.
  3. Compare supervised BC updates with RL updates for a generative policy; do not assume PPO log-probabilities are trivial to obtain.

Connect it to code

Train a tiny multimodal action denoiser or flow field on synthetic two-route data. Inspect Diffusion Policy's state example and one openpi data adapter. A full VLA fine-tune is a stretch goal after the main research loop works.
