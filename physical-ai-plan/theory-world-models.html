Learned world models and imagination

What must a model predict for imagined training to improve physical behavior?

Research core16–20 study hoursConnected lab 08 →

What to understand

  • Dyna and World Models: interaction, learned simulation, planning and policy updates; PETS ensembles and MBPO synthetic replay.
  • PlaNet/RSSM posterior versus prior, reconstruction/predictive losses, reward and continuation; Dreamer actor learning versus online search.
  • DreamerV3 and TD-MPC2 as implementation routes; DINO-WM and V-JEPA 2-AC as action-conditioned visual-feature planning routes.
  • Cosmos platform lineage and Cosmos 3; distinguish video generation, forward dynamics, inverse dynamics, reasoning and policy checkpoints.
  • Cosmos Policy and DreamZero: actions plus predicted visual futures, direct execution versus planning, and what the papers establish about adaptation.
  • Compounding error, rollout horizon, model exploitation and the difference between a realistic video and useful action-conditioned dynamics.

What to derive or explain

  1. Write p(z[t+1] | z[t], a[t]) and an observation-conditioned posterior, explaining what is available at deployment.
  2. Explain the components of a latent world-model loss and what each can fail to preserve.
  3. Draw the computational graph for a frozen world model and a trainable actor; specify whether learning uses differentiable rollouts or a synthetic-transition replay buffer.
