Lab 08 / Week 3–4

An action-conditioned dynamics ensemble

Start with a transparent learned dynamics model; expose its failure at the new obstruction.

8 focused hoursImplementTheory companion →

The implementation sequence

  1. Fit three small models to delta-state transitions, with bootstrapped episode-level datasets.
  2. Train and evaluate on nominal and blocked transitions; preserve the training/development/test split.
  3. Compare one-step error with 5/10/20-step rollout errors and failure/success discrimination.
  4. Add a frozen visual encoder and latent dynamics if the sensor pathway and compute permit. Record this as a separate observation setting.

How to verify it

  • An action permutation or zero-action intervention changes predictions appropriately.
  • The model retains the blocker and distinguishes collisions from progress on development cases.
  • All ensemble members are independently initialized; disagreement is not claimed to be a calibrated confidence interval.

What to produce

Paired predicted/executed trajectories, multi-step error curves and a documented model limitation.
