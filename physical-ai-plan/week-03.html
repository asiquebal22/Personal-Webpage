Week 03 / Foundations

Build the base policy and the first predictive model

Get the smallest scientifically useful policy/world-model pair running. Maintain the same task across learning exercises and research.

42 core hours
14 h Theory10 h Coding labs14 h Research4 h Interview / career

The daily plan

Day 1 starts on your selected program date; the labels are work sessions, not fixed weekdays. Divide each session using the week’s hour ledger.

DAY 01

Define training, development/acquisition and held-out test layout IDs before collection. Generate a modest simulation demonstration set. Measure real reset/labeling time before committing to hardware episode counts.

DAY 02

Train the nominal BC controller, save normalization statistics, and evaluate closed-loop rollouts. Inspect failure videos rather than choosing checkpoints from training loss alone.

DAY 03

Fit a small delta-state dynamics ensemble. Keep obstacle pose and relevant contact state observable. Validate one-step transitions on the development split.

DAY 04

Read PETS and PlaNet: trace ensemble trajectory sampling and the recurrent stochastic latent-state model. Survey RT-1, RT-2 and Open X-Embodiment to connect scaled robot policies with their datasets.

DAY 05

Compare 5/10/20-step model predictions with actual simulator execution from the same start and actions. Explain why a useful predictor must respond to changed actions, not only reconstruct the scene.

DAY 06

Freeze the core experiment interface and choose compact actor plus learned dynamics. Document the upgrade path to visual latents or a pretrained world-action model without making it a dependency.

DAY 07

Recover or absorb overflow

Keep this day flexible. Optional reading or a delayed run can fit here; do not assume another full mandatory workday.
