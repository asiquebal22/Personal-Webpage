Lab 06 / Week 3

Behavior cloning and distribution shift

Establish what the policy can already do before you claim it improves in imagination.

6 focused hoursImplementTheory companion →

The implementation sequence

  1. Collect or generate 50–100 simple demonstration trajectories in simulation; choose hardware counts only after timing resets.
  2. Train a small MLP BC policy on state observations, or a frozen visual encoder plus policy head.
  3. Split by layout/episode and evaluate closed-loop completion, not frame-level action error alone.
  4. Introduce held-out blocker placements and classify failures into perception, planning, contact/control and missing-skill cases.

How to verify it

  • No held-out layout is used to choose checkpoints or acquisition weights.
  • Normalization uses the training split only.
  • The baseline succeeds on the nominal task often enough to make recovery meaningful.

What to produce

A BC checkpoint, dataset card and nominal-versus-obstructed evaluation grid.
