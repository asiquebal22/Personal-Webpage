Week 01 / Foundations

Establish the foundations and the experiment contract

Understand the robot-learning loop and start the research infrastructure immediately. Diagnose knowledge gaps instead of redoing material already mastered.

The daily plan

Day 1 starts on your selected program date; the labels are work sessions, not fixed weekdays. Divide each session using the week’s hour ledger.

DAY 01

Diagnose and scope

Spend 90 minutes on the self-assessment. Review MDPs/returns; specify one obstructed manipulation task, its action interface and success rubric. Create the experiment repository and environment manifest.

DAY 02

Bellman and blockage

Implement the blocked-gridworld example. Evaluate a frozen policy before and after a wall change, then replan. Write what changed: environment state, dynamics, policy or action sequence.

DAY 03

Geometry and commands

Implement planar FK/Jacobian checks. Review frame composition and calibration. Inspect the physical arm interface or simulator and record action units, joint limits and control frequency.

DAY 04

Policy gradients

Derive REINFORCE and a baseline. Work through a hand-computed trajectory. Install only the small educational stack in an isolated environment; identify GPU requirements for the research stack.

DAY 05

Task and recorder

Create/reset the simulated scene. Record a scripted policy episode and outcome. Read DayDreamer's problem/method/experiments and sketch how policy learning differs from MPC.

DAY 06

Integrate and explain

Replay an episode, export an initial manifest, and record a five-minute explanation of the full observation→policy→controller loop. Review the week's assumptions and unresolved hardware access issues.

DAY 07

Recover or absorb overflow

Keep this day flexible. Optional reading or a delayed run can fit here; do not assume another full mandatory workday.

The deliverables

Decision gate

A scripted controller can complete one nominal episode and produce replayable data. If hardware is unavailable, continue in simulation while arranging access; do not postpone the model-learning pilot.

Explain this without notes

Why does a blockage sometimes require replanning, sometimes a policy update, and sometimes a better world model?

Record a short answer, identify where your reasoning is vague, and revisit only those gaps.
