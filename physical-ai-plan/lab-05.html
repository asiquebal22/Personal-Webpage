Lab 05 / Week 1–3

Robot interface and episode recorder

Create the shared interface every baseline will use. Finish a simulator episode before extensive collection.

6 focused hoursBuild for projectTheory companion →

The implementation sequence

  1. Define observe(), act(), reset() and step() with frame, units, timestamps and frequency documented.
  2. Record observations, executed actions, raw policy outputs, rewards, termination, collision/contact events and scene IDs.
  3. Save an episode manifest including calibration/configuration/checkpoint hashes and source = real, simulator or imagined.
  4. Replay the log offline; identify dropped frames, timestamp inversions and action saturation.

How to verify it

  • A second reader can load and visualize an episode without launching the robot.
  • Commanded and executed actions are distinguished.
  • All aborted attempts remain in the cost ledger; none silently disappear from outcome statistics.

What to produce

One interface, one validated episode schema and an inspection video.
