Research Summary

My research focuses on developing generalizable AI and machine learning methods for scientific discovery — including generative modeling (flow matching, diffusion), post-training via reinforcement learning (PPO, DPO), model interpretability, causal inference, and physics-informed models — applied to health care, materials science, and complex physical systems under uncertainty.

Fundamental and applied work on these topics appears in venues such as NAACL, IEEE TPAMI, IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing, and JAIR, and has earned finalist or winner recognition in eight best-paper competitions across IEEE, INFORMS, and IISE. This research has been supported by over $11M from NSF, NIH, DOD, and industry partners.

Research

My group builds AI methods that are rigorous enough to be trusted in scientific and engineering decision-making, and general enough to transfer across domains.

Generative AI for the Sciences

Flow matching, diffusion models, and adjoint matching for molecular and materials discovery; fine-tuning molecular foundation models in data-scarce regimes; emulating microstructural evolution with tensor-decomposed neural architectures.

Causal Inference & Interpretability

Counterfactual explanation methods (SCGAN) for clinical prediction; root-cause analysis via the velocity fields of optimal transport; interpretable models for treatment-response and diagnostic settings.

Physics-Informed & Uncertainty-Aware Learning

Discovering governing physics under uncertainty; stochastic inverse modeling for elastoplastic property characterization; change detection in complex dynamical systems.

Sequential Decision Making & Active Learning

Dynamic exploration–exploitation trade-offs with Bayesian hierarchical models; accelerating point-based value iteration for POMDPs; information-theoretic, surprise-driven approaches to decision support.

AI for Manufacturing & Digital Twins

Computational additive manufacturing, self-organizing networks for thermal simulation, autonomous laser processing, and vector quantization of sensor streams under bandwidth and edge-hardware constraints.

Applications in Health Care

Prediction of genetic mutations from -omics and imaging data (with Mayo Clinic), breast cancer risk and treatment-response modeling, and decision-support systems for clinicians.
